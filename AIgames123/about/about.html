About AIgames123

A free home for games made with AI.

AIgames123 is a free, community-driven archive where anyone can upload and play games created with the help of AI tools — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others. No installs, no sign-up to play, just click and go.

Why we built this

Over the past couple of years, a strange and wonderful thing happened: making a playable video game stopped requiring years of programming experience. With a few well-crafted prompts, anyone can now ask an AI model to write a complete, self-contained browser game — a maze, a shooter, a trading simulator, an idle clicker — and have something playable in minutes.

The problem was that all these little creations had nowhere to live. People were building genuinely fun, weird, and creative games in a single afternoon, then losing them in a chat history or a forgotten file. We wanted a place where these games could be shared, played, and discovered — a public arcade for the AI era.

That's AIgames123. Upload the HTML file your AI tool produced, and it's instantly playable by anyone with a browser. No build steps, no app stores, no gatekeepers.

What you'll find here

  • Instant-play games — every game runs directly in your browser, on desktop or mobile, with no download required.
  • A huge variety — because the games come from the community, you'll find everything from polished arcade games to delightfully strange experiments.
  • The prompts behind them — on our Prompts page, we share the actual AI prompts used to create some of the games, so you can learn and build your own.
  • Guides and tutorials — our blog walks you through making your own AI games from scratch.

How it works

Every game on AIgames123 is a self-contained web file that runs inside a sandboxed frame in your browser. That sandboxing is important: it means a game can't access your data or interfere with the rest of the site. You can play anonymously, upvote the games you enjoy, and report anything that doesn't belong. To upload your own game, you'll create a free account.

Our approach to quality and safety

Because anyone can upload, we take moderation seriously. Every game can be reported by users, and we review reports through an internal moderation queue. We don't allow sexually explicit material, graphic violence, hate speech, malware, or content that infringes someone else's copyright. You can read the full rules in our Terms of Service, and our DMCA page explains how rights-holders can request takedowns.

We also care about your privacy. We don't sell your data or use invasive advertising trackers. Our Privacy Policy lays out exactly what we collect and why.

Who runs it

AIgames123 is an independent project built and maintained by a solo developer who loves both games and the creative possibilities of AI. It isn't backed by a big company — it's a passion project that aims to stay free, fast, and fun.
